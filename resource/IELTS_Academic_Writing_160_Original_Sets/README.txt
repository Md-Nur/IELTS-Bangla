IELTS ACADEMIC WRITING — 160 ORIGINAL PRACTICE SETS

Each set includes both Academic Writing tasks:
- Task 1 visual-information report: bar chart, line graph, table, pie-chart data, process diagram, or map/plan comparison.
- Task 2 essay: opinion, discussion, advantages/disadvantages, problem/solution, or evaluation prompts.
- Timed instructions, word-count minimums, planning cues, and a self-checklist covering the four IELTS writing assessment criteria.

Official format: 60 minutes total; Task 1 about 20 minutes and at least 150 words; Task 2 about 40 minutes and at least 250 words. Task 2 carries greater weight in the overall Writing score.

These are original practice prompts, not official IELTS questions and not affiliated with IELTS, IDP, the British Council or Cambridge. Some chart structures and prompt types recur with changed topics/data. Task 1 information is provided as text/tables rather than rendered graphics. This is a broad practice bank, not 160 professionally calibrated official-equivalent tests; it does not provide model essays or guarantee a band score.
